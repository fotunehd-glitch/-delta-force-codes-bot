About the Delta Force Random Kit

Delta Force Random Kit is a free random loadout generator, or loadout randomizer, for Delta Force's Operations extraction mode. Hit Spin all to roll a full random kit: one of 17 operators, a map, a primary weapon or sidearm, and a helmet, armor vest, chest rig and backpack. Lock any slot with Hold and reroll the rest, set the tier range for each piece of gear, and turn off weapon classes, maps or operators you don't want.

Operators and weapons are shuffled so you see every option before anything repeats. Spin the wheels for a difficulty (Easy, Normal or Hard), a weapon mod budget and an ammo level (LV 1 to LV 6) to make each raid a challenge. The page also shows today's door codes for Zero Dam, Layali Grove, Brakkesh, Space City, Tide Prison and AZ3 Nuclear Plant, refreshed every few minutes.

How the Delta Force loadout randomizer works

  • Random operator: picks from every Operations operator, including the Season 11 additions. Untick any you don't own yet.
  • Random weapon: rolls a gun from the classes you choose: assault rifles, SMGs, marksman rifles, snipers, light machine guns, shotguns, special weapons and pistols.
  • Random gear: a gear randomizer for your helmet, armor, chest rig and backpack, with a tier range (grey to red) for each slot.
  • Random map: Zero Dam, Layali Grove, Brakkesh, Space City, Tide Prison or AZ3 Nuclear Plant.
  • Spin the wheel: three roulette wheels decide your difficulty, weapon mod budget and ammo level. Untick any option to take it off the wheel.

Random loadout challenge ideas

  • Full random: Spin all once, no rerolls, and try to extract with whatever you get.
  • Budget build: spin the budget wheel and only mod your weapon within that amount.
  • Grey ammo run: tick only LV 1 on the ammo wheel and see how far it takes you.
  • Full send: load the Full send gear preset, spin Hard difficulty and go for the red loot.
  • Squad roulette: everyone in your squad spins their own kit and nobody is allowed to swap.
